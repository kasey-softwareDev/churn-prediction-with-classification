# Telco Customer Churn Prediction & Analytics Pipeline

## Overview

This project analyses customer churn in a telecommunications dataset and builds classification models to identify customers who are likely to leave the business. Churn prediction is important because retaining existing customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.

The project compares **Logistic Regression** and **Random Forest** models, evaluates their performance, and interprets the results from a business-retention perspective.

---

## Business Objective

The goal is to help a telecom company identify customers at risk of churn so that the business can take proactive retention actions, such as targeted offers, contract reviews, or service improvement campaigns.

Key questions explored:

- Which customer attributes are related to churn?
- Can machine learning identify likely churners?
- Which model provides the best balance between accuracy, precision, and recall?
- Which model is more useful for customer retention decisions?
